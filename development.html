The Seven Cures for a Lean Purse

the chapter gave me 7 lessons to start building my personal wealth, these lessons were to save atleast 10% of what i earn, to not spend my money on wants and learn the difference between that and needs, to find sensible ways for my money to grow, to not use investments i dont trust, to build value rather than just buying a random place, to prepare for the future and to continue honing my skills so i myself can be valuable. my take away of this chapter is that i am responsible for my own wealth and focusing on material values and not long term values depletes my own worth

The Five Laws of Gold

the 5 laws of gold teaches about investments and how to grow the money i have saved (most likely continuing my lesson from the last chapter), the laws are namely: to not spend everything i make, to use my savings to generate more money, to seek ot advice about investments, to not put my money into an investment blindly, and to think realistically about how it would grow. this really highlights the importance of knowledge of how money works and how quickly you're able to lose said money

My biggest takeaway

overall this book is interesting as it allows people to learn how to not just make money and spend it on what they want but to learn about the money they recieve so that they may grow it and not deplete what they have and may, in the future, be stable enough to live freely
